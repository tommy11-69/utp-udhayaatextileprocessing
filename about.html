Our Journey

From our foundation to becoming a premier export-grade processing partner, our growth has been driven by technical innovation and client trust.

Foundation

Established in Erode, Tamil Nadu. Began operations with a core focus on fundamental woven fabric processing, building a reputation for reliability in the domestic market.

1990s

Expansion of Capacity

Scaled operations and integrated advanced continuous dyeing ranges. Began handling high-volume requirements for larger institutional suppliers and exporters.

2005

Technological Integration

Invested in advanced finishing machinery and aligned processes with global compliance standards (OEKO-TEX, GOTS). Established the dedicated in-house quality lab.

2015

A Trusted Export Partner

Today, Udhayaa is a premier processing partner handling specialized coatings, wide-width home textiles, and complex printed routes for a global client base.

Present

What We Do

We provide comprehensive greige-to-finished processing services, including high-quality solid dyeing, precision printing, and specialized coatings. We handle diverse woven fabrics intended for bed linens, bags, and home furnishings.
