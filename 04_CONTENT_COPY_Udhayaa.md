## 2. VOICE AND TONE

| Item | Decision |
|------|----------|
| **Voice in 3 words** | Professional · Established · Dependable |
| **We sound like** | "Experienced textile processing."<br>"Dependable fabric supply."<br>"Share your fabric specifications." |
| **We never sound like** | "We're the #1 world-class factory!"<br>"Check out our awesome new t-shirts!"<br>"100% defect-free magic fabric." |
| **Formality (1–5)** | 4 (Formal, credible B2B tone) |
| **Person** | "We / you" |
| **Sentence length** | Short, scannable, fact-driven. |
| **Jargon policy** | Use correct textile terms (greige, solid-dyed, MOQ) expected by B2B buyers. |
