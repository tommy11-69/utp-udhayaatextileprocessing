- **Type of offer:** B2B Services (Textile processing and processed-fabric supply).
- **Full product/service list:** 
  1. Greige-to-finished fabric processing
  2. Solid-dyed fabrics
  3. Printed fabrics
  4. Other finished-fabric requirements (subject to verification)
- **Hero products/services:** Solid-dyed and Printed fabrics.
- **Customisation:** Yes, entirely bespoke based on customer fabric, color/print, and finishing specifications.
- **What they do NOT sell:** Direct-to-consumer apparel, T-shirts, caps, hoodies, uniforms, buying-house sourcing services. Does NOT claim in-house dyeing machinery.
